Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Honeycomb Geoguesser (“the game”) is operated by Snail Games USA Inc (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at honeycomb@frozenway.games.

What we collect

  • Your Discord account information. You sign in to the game with Discord. When you do, Discord shares your Discord user ID, username, display name, profile picture link, and the email address on your Discord account with us. We never see your Discord password.
  • Your leaderboard name. Your Discord username is used as your leaderboard name and is shown publicly. Anyone who sees it may be able to find your Discord profile.
  • Your email preference. Whether you have chosen to receive Honeycomb news and updates by email, and when you opted in or out.
  • Gameplay information. Your scores, the speed you played, where you placed your pins, how many games you’ve played, and when you played.
  • Moderation records. If an account is banned for breaking our Terms, we keep a record of the ban and the reason.
  • Technical information. Like any website, when your browser loads the game, our service providers receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type. They may keep it in their logs for security and operation.

What we don’t collect

We don’t collect passwords, payment details, phone numbers, or your device’s location. We don’t read your Discord messages, servers, or friends list. The game has no advertising and uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your scores connected to your account across devices.
  • To run the game, show your results, and display public leaderboards.
  • To keep the game fair, for example by limiting how many games can be started in an hour, and to enforce our Terms, including banning accounts.
  • If you opt in, to send you Honeycomb news and updates by email.

We don’t sell your information or share it with anyone else for their own marketing.

Honeycomb news emails

We only send news and updates by email if you tick the box asking for them. You can unsubscribe at any time with the Unsubscribe link in the game or in any email we send. We keep a record of when you opted in or out so we can respect your choice. Opting out doesn’t affect your ability to play.

Information stored in your browser

When you sign in, the game stores a sign-in token in your browser’s local storage so you stay signed in, along with your preferred screenshot speed. This is needed for the game to work. Signing out or clearing your browser data removes it; your scores stay connected to your Discord account.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the game. They process information on our behalf or under their own privacy policies:

  • Discord: signs you in and shares the account information described above.
  • Supabase: sign-in, database, and file storage (account information, scores, game data, images). Data is stored on servers in Canada.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts: provides the typeface used on the site.
  • jsDelivr: delivers a code library the game uses.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information and gameplay information for as long as the game is running, unless you ask us to delete it. Leaderboards may be reset from time to time. We keep records of email opt-ins and opt-outs, and of bans, for as long as needed to respect your choice or enforce the ban. Our service providers keep their technical logs according to their own policies.

Your choices and rights

  • Email: opt in or out at any time in the game.
  • Access, correction, or deletion: email [contact email] from the address on your Discord account, or tell us your Discord username, and we’ll show you, correct, or delete the information connected to your account.
  • Disconnecting Discord: you can remove the game’s access in Discord under User Settings, Authorized Apps. This stops future sign-ins but doesn’t delete what we already hold, so contact us if you want that deleted too.

Depending on where you live, for example in the European Union, the United Kingdom, or California, you may have additional rights under local law, and you can contact us to exercise them.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and signing in requires a Discord account, which Discord only allows for people 13 and older (or older in some countries). We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has used the game, contact us and we’ll delete their information.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Some browsers send “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control” signals. The game doesn’t track you across other websites, doesn’t show ads, and doesn’t sell or share your personal information, so there is nothing for these signals to switch off and the game works the same either way. The service providers listed above receive standard technical information when your browser loads files from them, but we don’t use them to follow your activity over time or across other sites.

California privacy rights

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) gives you rights over your personal information. In the past 12 months, we have collected these categories of personal information:

  • Identifiers: your Discord user ID, username, display name, email address, and IP address (received by our service providers).
  • Internet or other electronic network activity: your gameplay information, such as scores, pin placements, and when you played.

We collect this information from you, from Discord when you sign in, and from your browser. We use it for the purposes described in “How we use it” above and disclose it only to the service providers listed above, to run the game. We don’t sell or share personal information (as the CCPA defines those terms), including that of consumers under 16, and we don’t collect sensitive personal information. We keep information as described in “How long we keep it” above.

You have the right to:

  • know what personal information we have collected about you and how we use and disclose it;
  • request a copy of that information;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • not be treated differently for exercising any of these rights.

To make a request, email [contact email]. To confirm the request is yours, we’ll ask you to email us from the address on your Discord account or to confirm details about your account. You can also have an authorized agent make a request for you with your written permission. We will respond within 45 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. We’ll update the date at the top when we do.
